# SolarLoss Phase2 — executive decision

**Retrospective shading screening works on the documented interventions, but the selected primary detector remains LIMIT because healthy false alarms exceed its frozen gate.** Scientific recommendation: demonstrate measured evidence and an investigation workflow; do not claim deployment-ready diagnosis, recovery or commercial savings.

1. **Can it detect physical shading?** 23/23 grouped episodes detected on 23 distinct days, across every documented class. All 23 have >=80% energy support. No missed groups. These are retrospective outcomes on one installation, not universal100% accuracy. All simple baselines also detect23/23. A day bootstrap gives degenerate recall percentiles [1,1] because every sampled day succeeds; this provides no useful population lower-bound guarantee.
2. **False alarms?** Primary weather detector: 153 healthy alert runs over 1794.51 supported hours = **0.0853/hour**, about one per 11.7 evaluated daylight hours. Exploratory day-bootstrap range 0.0719–0.0998/hour. Healthy window alert fraction 1.73%. The gate was <=0.05/hour, so E2 LIMIT. These are zero-labelled conditions; unrecorded faults cannot be ruled out. OLS benchmark 0.0444/hour, without post-test reselection.
3. **Meaningful generation deviation?** Known-event signed and positive-only deviations are estimable over eligible intervals. Demo signed deviation **0.397 kWh** with 98.28% observed labelled coverage. Its empirical model-error band is 0.370–0.395 kWh, based on 10 matched healthy blocks/7 days; this is not a true-counterfactual-loss confidence interval. Only10/23 events have sufficient matched placebos. E3 LIMIT: absolute effect accuracy is unverified.
4. **Does weather outperform?** Healthy selection MAE improves 9.07% versus OLS; later common healthy MAE is 13.03 W versus 13.57 W, 3.98% lower. Weather has more positive later bias and more healthy alarms. Better healthy regression error does not establish a better detector.
5. **Useful electrical signals?** Va provides operating-point context; demo matched Va difference is -24.55 V below its healthy comparator and 84.4% of supported event bins leave the healthy envelope. Ia is descriptive and class-dependent; demo shift is small. Ig tracks AC output and is not independent confirmation. Vg/Fg are contextual measurements without validated grid-fault ground truth. E4 LIMIT; universal cause diagnosis REJECT.
6. **Responsible technician checks?** Review acquisition quality, inverter status/MPPT/curtailment logs, independently verified irradiance, and safely obtained obstruction/soiling inspection evidence. Qualified PV personnel decide on further string measurements, IV testing or thermography under site/OEM requirements. E5 is a source-backed proposal only, not a successful diagnostic engine.
7. **Unproven?** Prospective transfer, fleet accuracy, cause classification, a perfectly healthy reference, exact unshaded inverter1 counterfactual, repair recovery, savings and real maintenance outcomes. No threshold/model choice changes were made after protected results.
